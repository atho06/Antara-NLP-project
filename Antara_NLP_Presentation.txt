# Antara: Your Safe Space to Talk
##################################################

Antara is a self-help emotional wellness companion app.

--- Core Pillars: ---
- 🤖 Lia — An empathetic AI conversational agent

##################################################
# 🤖 Lia — The Heart of Antara
##################################################

--- What is Lia? ---
An empathetic, context-aware conversational AI agent that responds to user messages in real time.

--- How it works: ---
- Powered by Llama 3.2:3b running locally via Ollama
- Receives conversation history + user message
- Generates empathetic, non-directive responses
- Falls back to curated built-in acknowledgments (no internet needed)

--- NLP Techniques: ---
| Technique | Implementation |
|-----------|---------------|
| Contextual language generation | Local LLM (transformer-based) |
| Prompt engineering | System prompt templates for therapeutic tone |
| Conversation memory | Multi-turn context window |
| Emotion-aware responses | Tone guided by crisis/emotion pipeline output |

Slide 6 — NLP Component 2: Crisis Detection

##################################################
# 🚨 Crisis Detection System
##################################################

--- Why it matters: ---
If a user expresses thoughts of self-harm or suicide, the app must respond immediately.

--- How it works: ---
`
User Input → Regex Pattern Matching → Negation Check → Crisis Score → Trigger/Dismiss
`

--- NLP Techniques Used: ---
- Regular Expressions — Keyword and phrase matching for crisis signals
  - "want to die", "end it all", "can't go on", "hurt myself" etc.
- Negation Handling — Prevents false positives
  - "I don't want to die" → ✅ NOT a crisis trigger
- Severity Scoring — Classifies mild concern vs. acute crisis
- Safety Circuit Breaker — Halts normal conversation; shows crisis resources

--- Output: ---
- 🟡 Low severity → Gentle check-in prompt
- 🔴 High severity → Crisis resource display + Emergency SMS dispatch

Slide 8 — NLP Component 4: Multi-Layer Analysis Pipeline
==================================================

##################################################
# 🧠 The NLP Analysis Pipeline
##################################################

Antara runs 4 parallel NLP analyzers on every user response:

---

--- 1. 🌫️ Hedging Detector (hedging_detector.py) ---
Detects linguistic patterns that minimize or dismiss emotional pain:
- "I guess I'm fine", "It's probably nothing", "I don't want to bother anyone"
- Flagging hedging → signals emotional suppression

---

--- 2. 📈 Emotion Trajectory Analyzer (emotion_analyzer.py) ---
Tracks the emotional arc across the entire conversation:
- Valence scoring (positive / negative affect)
- Peak distress detection
- Closing affect — is the user leaving better or worse?

---

--- 3. 🏷️ Theme Extractor (theme_extractor.py) ---
Keyword clustering to identify concern domains from free text:
- Groups keywords into semantic clusters (anxiety, loneliness, grief, etc.)
- Drives adaptive question flow (Slide 7)

---

--- 4. ⚡ Inconsistency Detector (inconsistency_detector.py) ---
Detects emotional masking — contradictory statements:
- "I'm totally fine, but I cry every morning"
- "I'm happy, but nothing feels worth doing"
- Flags mismatch between stated emotion and described behavior

Slide 9 — Emergency Safety Feature
==================================================

##################################################
# 🚨 Emergency Contact SMS System
##################################################

--- Scenario: ---
User is in acute distress. Crisis detection fires. The app dispatches an automated SMS to a pre-registered trusted contact.

--- Flow: ---
`
Crisis Detected → Severity Check → Retrieve Emergency Contact
→ Compose SMS → Dispatch via Twilio/Fast2SMS → Log to Database
`

--- SMS Content (auto-generated): ---
"Hi [Contact Name], your friend [User Name] may need your support right now. They've been using the Antara wellness app and have shown signs of distress. Please reach out to them when you can. — Antara Safety System"

--- Key Design Decisions: ---
- 📋 Logged in SQLite emergency_alerts table with timestamp
- 🔔 Consent-based — user registers contact voluntarily at sign-up
- 🌐 Offline fallback — simulated dispatch if no Twilio credentials
- 📵 Never reveals private conversation content in the SMS

Slide 11 — Privacy-First Architecture
==================================================

##################################################
# 🔒 Privacy by Design
##################################################

--- Why privacy matters in a mental health app: ---
Mental health data is extremely sensitive. Users must trust the system completely.

--- How Antara protects users: ---
| Concern | Our Approach |
|---------|-------------|
| 🤖 LLM data | Local Ollama — no data sent to OpenAI or any cloud |
| 🗄️ Storage | SQLite — data stays on the user's machine |
| 📡 Network | Only SMS dispatch (opt-in); no telemetry |
| 🔑 Auth | Session-based login; no OAuth or third-party SSO |
| 📋 Transparency | App clearly states it is non-diagnostic |

This is a research/educational prototype. In production, additional security measures (encryption at rest, audit logs, HIPAA compliance) would be required.

# ⚖️ Ethics & Responsibility
##################################################

--- What Antara is NOT: ---
- ❌ Not a clinical diagnostic tool
